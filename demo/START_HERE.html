What was reproduced?

The project evaluates released model checkpoints and reproduces analyses from saved outputs. It does not repeat the paper's large-scale SFT or PPO training. Original code, prompts and model responses remain unchanged.

Observed scores depend on output budget and answer finalization/extraction. Additional tokens helped some responses finish, but four RL responses still hit the 16k cap. Longer generation did not guarantee correctness. These observations do not establish that RL intrinsically reduces reasoning ability.

Completion and correctness are different

ColdStart ID2911 completes with a correct boxed answer at 16k. RL ID598 changes from an unfinished 8k candidate accepted by the scorer to an incorrect final boxed answer at 16k. RL IDs334, 612, 1118 and 2816 still hit the cap. A naturally finished response, a complete boxed answer and a correct official score are separate measurements.

The 8k views are trajectory prefixes, not independent requests or continuations of the original 100-question outputs. This follow-up does not replace the original scores. No original Qwen answers are combined with it into a new 20-question model comparison.

RL has longer observed reasoning on 17 of 20 paired student questions. Four RL reasoning sections remain right-censored at the 16k cap; its mean is therefore an observed lower bound. Teacher generation stopping status is unavailable.

The teacher sample contains text tasks, while students answer visual questions. These statistics do not establish stronger reasoning, faithful CoT, visual attention or teacher-to-student capability transfer.

Sampling and trace provenance

The teacher sample is a uniform draw without replacement from 1,637,924 rows across five verified shards, seed 20261026. The fixed 1,000 raw traces and the tokenizer are included, so recounting does not require re-downloading the full teacher dataset. Student traces are reused from the completed budget follow-up; no new generation is needed.
